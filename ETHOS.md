# PREOS Ethos

1. Production readiness is proved by current evidence, not confidence or conversation memory.
2. UNKNOWN is a first-class state and never silently becomes GREEN.
3. The 75 controls are a deterministic minimum, never replaced by contextual risk generation.
4. Risk is generated throughout the lifecycle, not once at project start.
5. Architecture is a hypothesis under current constraints. Every major decision has economics, migration and review triggers.
6. Deferred complexity without a measurable activation trigger is invalid.
7. Evidence expires or becomes stale when its bound code, configuration, environment, schema, dependency or assumption changes.
8. Dependency failures contaminate downstream control claims. A dependent control cannot remain GREEN on a RED or UNKNOWN prerequisite.
9. AI may analyze, propose, implement approved scope, test, measure and escalate. AI may not accept material risk or approve its own exception.
10. Human role gaps remain explicit ROLE GAPs. An AI persona cannot impersonate professional, executive or legal authority.
11. Incidents, telemetry, support findings and cost anomalies must feed back into risks, tests, monitors, runbooks and architecture review.
12. PREOS, the Blueprint and gstack remain separate repositories or installed systems, but they operate as one production delivery system through explicit contracts.
