# Integrated Lifecycle

1. Idea and discovery through the Blueprint, using `gstack-office-hours` when applicable.
2. Product challenge through `gstack-plan-ceo-review`.
3. PRD baseline and Project Classification.
4. Blueprint selects PREOS assurance level and maturity/threat context.
5. `$preos-project-init` compiles the hash-bound Project Contract.
6. `$preos-risk-model` performs risk Pass A.
7. Architecture plus `gstack-plan-eng-review` and `$preos-architecture-economics`.
8. Design, SRS, SRD, threat model, canonical data/API contracts and tests.
9. `$preos-risk-model` performs architecture Pass B.
10. Before each substantial change, change-impact analysis and risk delta.
11. `$preos-production-plan` enriches the approved Blueprint AI Task Packet.
12. Human approval of consequential scope and risk decisions.
13. `$preos-production-implement` routes bounded Codex implementation.
14. `gstack-review`, `gstack-cso`, `gstack-qa`, `gstack-qa-only`, `gstack-benchmark`, or other specialists as triggered.
15. PREOS release-risk aggregation and G0-G11 evaluation.
16. Human production approval.
17. `gstack-ship`, `gstack-land-and-deploy`, `gstack-canary` when authorised.
18. Production telemetry, incidents, support, cost and security findings.
19. `$preos-production-learn` converts accepted lessons into risks, regression tests, monitors, runbooks, ADR review triggers and evidence invalidations.
20. `gstack-retro` and Blueprint change control begin the next governed iteration.
